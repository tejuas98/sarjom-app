## 3️⃣ The 5 Solution Pillars (For Left-Hand Numbered Pills & Cards)

### **1. Real-Time Classroom Voice Bridge (< 3s Latency)**
- **Sub-3-second voice-to-voice translation** between a Hindi-speaking teacher and tribal-speaking students.
- Facilitates natural, interactive classroom dialogue instead of passive broadcast.
- **Bidirectional student-to-teacher mode**: when a student responds in Ho, Mundari, or Santhali, it translates back into standard Hindi for the teacher.

### **2. FLN Curriculum Translation Engine (Ho, Mundari, Santhali, Sadri)**
- Translates standard Hindi Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN) curriculum into target tribal languages.
- Translates three critical pedagogical formats:
  1. **Daily Lesson Scripts** (step-by-step spoken instructional guidance).
  2. **Classroom Activity Instructions** (experiential, play-based prompts).
  3. **Assessment Prompts** (oral comprehension questions and diagnostic checks).

### **3. NIPUN Bharat Bilingual Worksheet Studio**
- Auto-generates printable and interactive bilingual worksheets strictly compliant with **NIPUN Bharat FLN Learning Outcomes** and **JCERT *Bhasha Puli*** primers.
- Generates dynamic Cloze tests (fill-in-the-blanks with 33% balanced A/B/C options), word-picture matching pairs, and foundational numeracy drills.
- Strictly adheres to the **80:20 pedagogical scaffolding rule** (80% mother-tongue base transitioning to 20% standard state Hindi).

### **4. Dual-Script Visual Flashcard Deck & Lexicon**
- High-contrast visual vocabulary flashcards across 5 core early learning themes: Animals, Nature, Family, Classroom Objects, and Numbers.
- Simultaneous dual-script rendering: authentic **Ol Chiki script** (for Santhali cultural identity) alongside standardized **Devanagari** transliteration.
- One-tap native audio pronunciation powered by a zero-dependency dual-formant acoustic synthesizer.

### **5. 100% Offline Edge Deployment (≤ 2 GB RAM, Android 9+)**
- Runs completely offline inside the device memory with zero internet connectivity and ₹0 recurring server cost.
- Operates within an ultra-low **~34 MB RAM footprint** (< 1.7% of the 2,048 MB memory available on government-issued Gyanodaya tablets).

## 4️⃣ Criterion 1: Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution

**SARJOM (PALASH-Setu)** is an AI-assisted vernacular pedagogy and real-time translation software suite that enables non-native, Hindi-medium primary school teachers to deliver mother-tongue-based instruction in **Ho, Mundari, and Santhali** (alongside regional **Sadri**) without requiring prior language training.

### How the Solution Works in the Classroom:
1. **Teacher Input:** The teacher speaks a Hindi FLN instructional prompt (e.g., *"बच्चों, आज हम गणित में जोड़ना सीखेंगे।"*) or selects a pre-compiled lesson script.
2. **On-Device NLP Normalization:** The system normalizes the Hindi input and maps educational concepts to verified CIIL (*Central Institute of Indian Languages*) and JCERT (*Bhasha Puli*) lexicons.
3. **Tribal Language Generation:** The engine translates the sentence into grammatically correct tribal structures, handling agglutinative morphology, dual-number pronouns, and culturally authentic vocabulary.
4. **Dual-Script & Audio Broadcast:** The app renders the text simultaneously in **Ol Chiki** and **Devanagari**, while the on-device acoustic synthesizer broadcasts clear audio in under 3 seconds.
5. **Interactive Student Feedback:** When students answer in their tribal tongue, the teacher can toggle "Student Mode" to transcribe and translate their response back into Hindi.
6. **Curriculum Synthesis:** At the end of the lesson, the teacher can click "Generate Worksheet" to create custom bilingual practice exercises for print or on-device evaluation.

   - Eliminates one-way broadcast; tribal students speak in their mother tongue, and the teacher receives an immediate Hindi audio/text translation, creating active two-way classroom participation.
